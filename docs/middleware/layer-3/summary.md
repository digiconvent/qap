# Layer 3: built from atomic parts

Layer 3 holds the parts the intents are built from: deciding a submission, settling it once, carrying it out through its endpoint or rejecting it, recording a withdrawal, its deadline timer, keeping and cleaning up submissions, eligibility and the voter count. They combine the atomic parts of layer 4 with what the host supplies.
