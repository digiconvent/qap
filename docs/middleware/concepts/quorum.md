# C6 Quorum

The quorum is the number of people who must take part before a [submission](submission.md) can be approved. Taking part means voting in favour or against; withholding does not count. Without a quorum, the favour [Threshold](threshold.md) alone decides. A quorum never stops a submission from being rejected.

---

**Kind:** concept.

Optional. A whole number, read like a [Threshold](threshold.md): positive is a number of people, negative is a percentage of the eligible voters. [Poll evaluation](../layer-3/poll-evaluation.md) compares it, through [Required count](../layer-4/required-count.md), with the participation from [Vote count](../layer-4/vote-count.md).
